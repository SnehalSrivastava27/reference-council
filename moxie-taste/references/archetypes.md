# Creator Archetypes

Nine archetypes observed across the reference set. Assign exactly one per
creator. The orchestrator uses this list to enforce roster spread — no archetype
should exceed a third of a campaign.

| Archetype | Reference | Pillar | Delivers | Typical pace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Identity Storyteller** | `ahillyeah`, `asmitapathik` | 1 | Emotional depth, underrepresentation angle, highest saves and shares | Slowest, 1.4–2.6s |
| **Skeptic Convert** | `srish_teee`, `nutellaonella` | 1 | Objection handling — "I thought only curly girls could do this" | 1.2–1.5s |
| **Routine Demonstrator** | `aashiadani`, `shrijajhinkwan` | 1 | Clean numbered steps. Most repeatable, best conversion | 1.0s |
| **Humidity Tester** | `taneesho`, `eshnakutty` | 1 | Travel-glam, tropical and monsoon proof, highest saturation | 1.1–1.4s |
| **Educator** | `globalbeautyfinds` | 1 | Ingredient breakdowns, Wavy Hair Index explainer, technique detail | 1.1s |
| **Makeover Host** | `vibhasree_` | 1 | Third-party proof — does someone else's hair, removes the self-interest problem | 1.2s |
| **Quiet Aesthete** | `theriyakohli` | 1 | No talking, minimal lowercase captions, pure demo. Mood over argument | 1.1s |
| **The Professional** | kitchen, dance studio films | 2 | Occupational stress test. Shortest, fastest, funniest | 0.7–1.0s |
| **The Founder** | office/warehouse film | 2 | Behind-the-scenes credibility, unboxing, offer communication | 1.7s |

## Roster construction

Storytellers carry reach; demonstrators carry conversion. A roster of all
storytellers gets saves and no sales. A roster of all demonstrators gets
tutorials nobody shares.

A balanced campaign of six looks roughly like: two Identity Storytellers, one
Routine Demonstrator, one Skeptic Convert, one Humidity Tester, one Professional.

Hold the reference set's pillar ratio of roughly 4:1 Testimony to Stress test
unless the campaign has a stated reason to shift it.

## Assignment rule

Assign based on what a creator already does well, not on what the roster is
short of. Forcing a Quiet Aesthete to carry an Identity narrative produces a
worse film than casting someone else for the gap.

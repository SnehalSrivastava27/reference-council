# Backgrounds and Lighting

## The five background archetypes

### 1. Soft wall, shallow depth
*Reference: `aashiadani`, `theriyakohli`, `ahillyeah`*

One painted wall — blush pink, beige, sage — plus a single plant or lamp.
Nothing else. Wall sits 1–2m behind, slightly soft.

**Lighting:** window camera-left at 45°. Wall picks up a gradient, which is what
stops it reading as a blank backdrop. Needs a second source or a bounce behind
her for the reveal, otherwise the hair goes flat against the wall.
**Risk:** the most over-used archetype. Check roster spread before choosing it.

### 2. Lived-in home with a doorway
*Reference: `shrijajhinkwan`, `srish_teee`, `nutellaonella`*

Frame includes a hallway, open door or second room. Ceiling fan, curtains, bed
edge. The receding plane is what sells "real home".

**Lighting:** the far room does the work — light spilling from a doorway behind
her gives free hair separation. The strongest single trick in the reference set.
**Risk:** dark wood doors. Dark hair against them loses all edge definition.

### 3. Outdoor and travel
*Reference: `eshnakutty`, `taneesho`, `asmitapathik`*

Balcony grills, terrace nets, palm fronds, resort greenery, beach.

**Lighting:** open shade or golden hour, never midday overhead sun. Measures
warmest and most saturated in the set — `eshnakutty` at 1.68 warmth and 0.46
saturation. Acceptable here because the location justifies it.
**Risk:** overexposure on the reveal, and saturation competing with the tube.

### 4. Workplace and craft
*Reference: kitchen, dance studio, office/warehouse films*

Commercial pastry kitchen with steel counters. Wood-floor dance studio with LED
strips. Brand office with a slogan wall and stacked yellow cartons.

**Lighting:** usually fluorescent overhead, which measures cool and flat.
Requires a warm practical or a window in frame to pull warmth back above 1.15.
The dance studio manages 1.35 because of the wood floor and warm LEDs.
**Risk:** overhead-only light. Add a source at eye level or move to a window.
**Why it works:** the location is the proof. Mandatory for Pillar 2.

### 5. Tight application framing
*Reference: all films*

Framing so close the background is irrelevant. Used to bridge between locations
and to carry the technique.

**Lighting:** move closer to the source. Wet hair swallows light — lift about a
third of a stop or these shots go muddy.

## The lighting rule that matters most

Hair needs a second source from behind or the side.

Flat frontal light flattens wave definition into a dark mass. Every film where
the clumps genuinely pop has light coming through or across the hair. If a
location cannot provide it, change the location rather than accepting the
compromise — this is the difference between footage that sells the product and
footage that doesn't.

## Targets

| Metric | Target | Fail state |
|---|---|---|
| Luma | 115–135 | Under 110 = wet shots go muddy |
| Warmth (R/B) | 1.20–1.35 | Under 1.15 = off-brand, no reference example exists |
| Saturation | 0.25–0.33 | Over 0.40 acceptable outdoors only |
| Luma SD | 8–20 single-location | Over 25 means the film travels — must be motivated |

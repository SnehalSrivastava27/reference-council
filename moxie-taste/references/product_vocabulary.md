# Product and Claims Vocabulary

## Hero SKUs

- Gentle Cleansing Shampoo
- Ultra Hydrating Conditioner
- Weightless Leave-in Conditioner
- Flexi Styling Serum Gel
- Wax Stick + Brush (Pillar 2)

## The frameworks

- **The Moxie Wavy Theory** — the narrative device. Presented as a card or
  booklet on screen.
- **The Wavy Hair Index** — the hair-type diagnostic, including the 2C spectrum
  card.

These are the load-bearing element of Pillar 1. Creators treat them as knowledge
that was withheld from them — a diagnosis, not a product benefit. Delivered as a
slogan, the film collapses into an ad.

## Claims language

Sulfate-free · silicone-free · paraben-free · vegan · no sticky finish · all-day
hold · salon-grade

Ingredient callouts as observed: mango butter + ginseng root extract; flax and
chia seed + brown seaweed extract.

## Technique dialect

This is the shared vocabulary across creators. Using it makes a script sound
native; substituting synonyms makes it sound written by a brand.

`pea-sized amount` · `on soaking wet hair` · `scrunch` · `scrunch again` ·
`don't touch it` · `let it air dry` · `lock everything in` · `hold, but not
stiff or crunchy` · `don't flatten the pattern` · `brushing it dry makes it
poofy`

## Proof environments

Claims are stress-tested against real conditions, never lab conditions:

Mumbai pollution · Indian humidity · monsoon frizz · Bali · Sri Lanka · a hot
commercial kitchen · 45 minutes of Bhangra

## End card

Yellow logo card, held static for the final 1–2 seconds. Tagline: for hair
that's got character.
